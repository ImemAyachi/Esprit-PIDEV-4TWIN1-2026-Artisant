# 🎬 Artisant Commercial Video Production Plan

This document contains the strategic plan, full script, and segmented AI prompts for the **Artisant** commercial video.

---

## 📜 Master Script
**Total Estimated Duration:** ~60-70 seconds

### [SCENE 1 — HOOK]
*   **Tone:** Curious, slightly serious
*   **Pace:** Slow
*   **Script:** "Did you know… (pause 0.5s) that thousands of Tunisian artisans… (pause 0.5s) still manage their entire business… (pause 0.7s) with pen… and paper?"

### [SCENE 2 — PROBLEM]
*   **Tone:** Building tension
*   **Pace:** Medium, slightly faster over time
*   **Script:** "An electrician wins a contract… (pause 0.4s) writes the quote by hand… (pause 0.4s) delivers it in person… (pause 0.4s) orders materials by SMS… (pause 0.4s) and waits… weeks… for payment. In a digital age… our construction sector… is still stuck in the past."

### [SCENE 3 — SOLUTION]
*   **Tone:** Relief, confident
*   **Pace:** Steady, clear
*   **Script:** "Artisant… is a unified digital platform… built for construction professionals. No more scattered tools. No more missed messages. No more lost paperwork. Everything you need… quotes… orders… invoices… coordination… in one single place. The chaos disappears. The work… flows."

### [SCENE 4 — VALUE]
*   **Tone:** Assertive, modern
*   **Pace:** Slightly faster
*   **Script:** "Before Artisant… disconnected tools… wasted hours… zero visibility. With Artisant… one platform… full control… and an AI agent… that finds what you need… in seconds."

### [SCENE 5 — IMPACT]
*   **Tone:** Inspiring, warm
*   **Pace:** Slower, emotional
*   **Script:** "When artisans work smarter… projects finish on time. Communities grow. A whole industry… transforms. Artisant doesn’t just help one worker… it builds a better future… for everyone."

### [SCENE 6 — CTA]
*   **Tone:** Confident, decisive
*   **Pace:** Strong finish
*   **Script:** "Be the professional… who builds smarter. Stay in control. Join Artisant… now."

## 🎥 12-Second AI Video Prompts
Use these prompts one-by-one in your AI generation tool (Sora, Runway, Luma).

### Clip 1: The Hook (0:00 - 0:12)
> **Prompt:** "Extreme close-up of a weathered hand holding a wooden pencil, writing slowly on a crumpled, dusty piece of yellowed paper. Dim, warm workshop lighting. Sawdust in the air. 4k, cinematic macro photography, shallow depth of field, 35mm lens."

### Clip 2: The Problem (0:12 - 0:24)
> **Prompt:** "Cinematic montage: A tired electrician in a workshop scribbling on a notepad, then walking through a busy street under the hot sun to deliver a letter. Fast-paced cuts. A phone screen showing a messy SMS thread with material orders. Darker, high-contrast grading."

### Clip 3: The Solution (0:24 - 0:36)
> **Prompt:** "A sudden transition to a clean, bright, modern digital interface. A sleek smartphone screen displays the 'Artisant' logo. Smooth animation of a unified dashboard with clean icons for 'Quotes', 'Orders', and 'Invoices'. Vibrant teal and white aesthetic."

### Clip 4: The Value & AI (0:36 - 0:48)
> **Prompt:** "High-tech visualization: A glowing 3D architectural model of a house rotating on a tablet. An AI chatbot interface slides in, scanning data instantly. Data particles flowing smoothly. Corporate tech style, clean, professional, bright lighting."

### Clip 5: The Impact (0:48 - 1:00)
> **Prompt:** "Heartwarming cinematic shot of a beautiful, finished modern building in Tunisia. An architect and a construction worker shaking hands and smiling. Golden hour lighting. Drone shot pulling back to show a thriving local community."

### Clip 6: The CTA (1:00 - 1:12)
> **Prompt:** "Dynamic shot of a professional worker walking confidently toward a modern construction site, holding a tablet showing the Artisant Dashboard. Smooth motion. Fade to a clean white background with the bold 'ARTISANT' logo reveal."

## 🛠️ Production Recommendations
*   **Voiceover:** Use **ElevenLabs** (Professional, Trustworthy Male/Female voice). Add 0.5s pauses as noted in the script.
*   **Background Music:** **Suno AI** prompt: "Ambient corporate tech, inspiring, upbeat, rhythmic construction percussion, minimalist synth, high production value."
*   **Editing:** Assemble clips in **CapCut** or **Premiere**, syncing the visual transitions with the change in "Tone" noted in each scene.
