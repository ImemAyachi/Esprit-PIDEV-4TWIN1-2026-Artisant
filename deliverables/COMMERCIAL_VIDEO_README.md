# 🎥 Commercial Video Production: Artisant (PI-CDIO)

This document serves as the official production guide and script for the **Artisant** commercial video, designed to meet the **CCCA4 PI / CDIO** evaluation criteria.

---

## 📋 Production Strategy

| Phase | Description | Timing |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Hook** | 0-5s: Capture attention. | 5s |
| **Problem** | 5-15s: Show the chaos. | 10s |
| **Solution** | 15-35s: Introduce Artisant. | 20s |
| **Added Value** | 35-45s: Why Artisant? | 10s |
| **Impact** | 45-55s: SDG & Community. | 10s |
| **CTA** | 55-60s: Closing identity. | 5s |

---

## 📜 Official Pitch Script (3:00)

### [0:00 - 0:05] The Hook
**Visual:** Close-up of a hand shaking while writing on a dusty piece of wood.
**Audio:** "Why is our most essential industry still stuck in the past?"

### [0:05 - 0:15] The Problem
**Visual:** Montage of a messy desk, lost receipts, and a stressed worker rubbing his temples.
**Audio:** "Scattered orders. Lost invoices. For Tunisian artisans, paperwork is a second job that pays nothing."

### [0:15 - 0:35] The Solution
**Visual:** Ahmed swipes a screen. A clean, 3D 'Artisant' dashboard appears. Ahmed looks relieved.
**Audio:** "Artisant brings the site and office together. Quotes, orders, and tracking in one unified stream. The chaos disappears. The work flows."

### [0:35 - 0:45] Added Value
**Visual:** Split screen: Old messy way vs. New clean Artisant way.
**Audio:** "Don't just store data. Use an AI partner that understands the Tunisian workflow and predicts your needs."

### [0:45 - 0:55] SDG & Impact
**Visual:** Drone shot of a finished building with diverse workers using tablets.
**Audio:** "By digitizing the artisan, we build a stronger, more sustainable economy for all."

### [0:55 - 1:00] Call to Action
**Visual:** Ahmed stands tall at the site. Artisant Logo reveal.
**Audio:** "Build smarter. Join Artisant today."


## 📝 Evaluation Checklist (PI-CDIO)
- [ ] **Hook:** Is it under 15s? Does it use a question?
- [ ] **Problem:** Is the emotion of "Stress/Chaos" visible?
- [ ] **Solution:** Is the "Shift → Relief → Control" formula followed?
- [ ] **SDG:** Is the impact on the community shown visually (not just spoken)?
- [ ] **CTA:** Is it identity-aligned ("Be this") rather than instruction-aligned ("Do this")?
